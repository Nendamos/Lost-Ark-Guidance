What Makes Your Character Stronger

A rough priority order for where to spend time and gold. The left column moves the needle the most — work down that list before chasing anything on the right.

Big Effect

  1. DPS Uptime (Raid Knowledge + Good Skill Rotation)
  2. Ark Grid (+17 Points to fully activate the Ark Grid Cores)
  3. Ark Grid Passive Effects (Boss Damage → Additional Damage → Atk. Power)
  4. Ark Passive and Karma (Reaching Rank 6)
  5. Good Engravings
  6. Weapon Honing (Aiming for +25)
  7. Armor Honing
  8. Skill Levels +13 or +14 (Solid damage increase to skills)
  9. High Engraving Quality (Relic)
  10. Weapon Quality
  11. Accessory Effect (Outgoing Damage 2%, Weapon Power 3%)
  12. Bracelet Stats
  13. Gems
  14. Card Set
  15. Proper Synergy

Small Effect

  • Skill Runes
  • Battle Items
  • Card Collection Bonus
  • Pet Effect
  • Skin Basic Effect (1% Epic, 2% Legendary skin)
  • Roster Level
  • Paradise Orb
  • Food

Horizontal Content

How it is calculated

Bonus (capped): max(min(speed, 140) - 100, 0) Excess: max(speed - 140, 0) Total speed: 100 + sum of bonuses BT AS/MS: (1 + 0.26/699 * Spec) * 20% Base: (bonusAS + bonusMS) * baseRate Excess: (overAS + overMS) * excessRate Total: min(base + flat + excess, maxEvo)
Level 1 base 5%, excess 15%, flat 4%, max 12%
Level 2 base 10%, excess 30%, flat 8%, max 24%
Why 26.67% and not 26.66%? At 140% on both speeds the base conversion gives 8% and the flat bonus gives 8%, so the excess has to provide the remaining 8%. That is 8 / 0.30 = 26.666...% of combined overcap. The calculator works in 0.01% steps and rounds each value down, so 26.66% would give 7.99% excess and a total of 23.99%, which is just short of the cap. 26.67% is the smallest overcap that gives the full 8.00% and reaches the 24% maximum. Level 1 ends up at the same number: (12 - 4 - 4) / 0.15 = 26.67%.

Calculation logic from the Supersonic Breakthrough Calculator by poyomi. Class bonuses are game values and can change with patches.

How it is calculated

Break-even: floor(0.95 * value / players * (players - 1)) Profit: floor(0.92 * break-even)
0.95 accounts for the 5% market fee you pay when you sell the item.
(players - 1) / players is the part of your bid that goes to the other players. You get your own share back.
0.92 keeps your bid 8% below break-even, so you still make a profit.
Each step rounds down to a whole gold amount.

Results checked against the Auction Calculator on Lost Ark Utils. Rules can change with patches.
